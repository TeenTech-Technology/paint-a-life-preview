About Us

Our difference is what makes us beautiful

Paint a Life aims at providing access to relevant information that identifies and embraces the uniqueness of children and youths, creating an environment that will nurture and grow their skills so that, together, we can create a better world.


More about Paint a Life

Our Mission

To strengthen the leadership of children and youths to thrive in all aspects of their lives.

Our Vision

To have a fully equipped and empowered generation of children and youths for personal and global development.

Join Our Community

Become a member, volunteer or partner

Paint a Life incorporates individuals, organisations and churches. Any individual, organisation or institution passionate about making a difference and bringing positive change that will impact a generation can join as a member or volunteer.


Join us Invite us for talks / workshops

Benefits of being a member

  1. Networking
  2. Job experience
  3. Local and International opportunities
  4. Sharpening and learning of various skills
  5. Expanding knowledge on different topics
  6. Being part of a team that is changing lives and making impact – be the change you want to see
