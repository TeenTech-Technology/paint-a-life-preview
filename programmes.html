Our Services

Three projects, one vision

Education, Mentorship and Advocacy for children and youths.

01 · Education

Legacy Project – Every contact leaves a trace

Empowerment through education with emphasis on Literacy development, Reproductive Health awareness and Child Sponsorship.

Identified community schools, children and youths are provided with necessary literature and information, promoting a reading culture that opens them up to more knowledge. The project also sponsors vulnerable children, nurturing their skill set and creating an awareness of what is in them, so they accept and appreciate their unique being: their difference is not a disadvantage or disability, but rather what makes them distinguished and influential in their own sphere.

02 · Mentorship

Leadership Project – Developing the mind of a leader

An empowerment project designed to raise the aspirations of children and youths, and transform them into leaders.

Delivered through Mentorship, Skills Development and Entrepreneurship trainings at conferences, workshops and seminars, giving children an in-depth understanding of cross-cutting issues affecting them, their communities and the world – so they make informed decisions at a young age and Dream it, Do it and Inspire others.

03 · Advocacy

Love Project – 1 John 4:19

Sharing the love and blessings we have received in Christ with less privileged children.

Through the provision of food stuffs, clothing, shoes etc.; advocacy campaigns that seek justice and create a safe environment for children and youths; and counselling services to those in need – in orphanages, clinics and under-served communities, embracing each child regardless of their social status.
