Our programmes

Paint a Life runs three projects in the fulfilment of its vision, delivered through Education, Mentorship and Advocacy.

Legacy Project

Every contact leaves a trace

Empowerment through education with emphasis on Literacy Development, Reproductive Health Awareness and Child Sponsorship – providing community schools, children and youths with literature and information, promoting a reading culture, and sponsoring vulnerable children. Their difference is not a disadvantage or disability; it is what makes them distinguished and influential in their own sphere.

Leadership Project

Developing the mind of a leader

Raising the aspirations of children and youths and transforming them into leaders through Mentorship, Skills Development and Entrepreneurship trainings at conferences, workshops and seminars – so they can make informed decisions young and Dream it, Do it and Inspire others.

Love Project

1 John 4:19

Sharing the love and blessings received in Christ with less privileged children through food stuffs, clothing and shoes; Advocacy campaigns that seek justice and a safe environment for children and youths; and counselling for those in need – in orphanages, clinics and under-served communities.
