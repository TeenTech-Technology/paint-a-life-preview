About Paint a Life Initiative

Paint a Life is a community of young people who are passionate about touching lives and sharing the love of Christ through Mentorship and Empowerment. We aim at providing access to relevant information that identifies and embraces the uniqueness of children and youths, and thus create an environment that will nurture and grow their skills so that when brought together, we can create a better world, because our difference is what makes us beautiful.

Founded in 2018 and registered in September 2019 with the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA). Motto: “Many Faces, One Voice”. Operational brand: “Equip, Empower, and Transform”.

‘The rainbow is only as colourful, bright and beautiful as the different colours added to it.’ — Louisa Mulemba

Mission

To strengthen the leadership of children and youths to thrive in all aspects of their lives.

Vision

To have a fully equipped and empowered generation of children and youths for personal and global development.

Reach

Primary target: boys and girls between 6 – 25 years old.

Our values

Integrity

To be truthful and honest in all our actions.

Accountability

We are personally accountable for effective delivery in our communities, because if it is to be, it is up to me.

Passion

Committed in heart and mind, to equip and empower children and youths.

Continuous learning

We are open-minded, willing and eager to learn at all times for the betterment of our work.

Ownership

Personal obligation of bringing about positive results, and not waiting for others to act.

Teamwork

Our success is dependent upon the collective energy, intelligence, and contributions of all our team members.
